Accounts heartbeat: format and rule

For a.maestro and Dinis. Each Claude account (cl-1 to cl-4, cl-6, more later) has a home repository, Site__Agent__Maestro__Claude__N, and its maestro commits site/heartbeat.json there. This page is the format, the rule and the code. Draft 2 takes a.maestro's answers: room is a status, not a percentage (sessions only see rate_limit_info's status); heartbeats come from the six Maestro homes (Claude 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8); business hours stay Monday to Friday 08:00 to 19:00 UK time until Dinis says otherwise.

1. The format

One small JSON file, replaced at every beat. No secrets: no tokens, keys, session links or private names. Example: heartbeat/example.json.

FieldMeaning
vFormat version, 1.
atWhen this beat was written, UTC, from date -u (YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ).
accountcl-1 and so on.
agentThe maestro that wrote it.
stateOne of working (work in hand), idle (nothing in hand), blocked (everything in hand waits on someone else), paused (Dinis paused this account on purpose), stopped (the account cannot work: out of room, signed out, broken).
room.statusFrom rate_limit_info (seven-day window): allowed, allowed_warning or rejected.
room.resets_atWhen the weekly window resets, UTC (resetsAt).
room.overagetrue when the account is using overage (isUsingOverage).
room.used_pctOptional: a percentage, only when Dinis gives one. Shown, never used in the rule.
work.working, work.blockedHow many of the account's sessions are working, and how many are blocked.
asks.count, asks.linkHow many open asks wait on Dinis, and one public link to where they are listed (0 and null when none).
next_byWhen the maestro will beat next, by the rule below. Shown on the page; the check works out its own due time and does not rely on this one.
noteOptional, one line, at most 140 characters, public-safe.

2. The rule: when the next beat is due

A maestro works out its next interval at each beat from the beat's own time and fields. The check uses the same sum, so both agree.

interval = base × work × room, never less than 30 minutes and never more than 12 hours.

FactorWhen (UK time, Europe/London)Value
baseBusiness hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 19:0030 min
baseDay outside business hours: 07:00 to 23:002 h
baseNight: 23:00 to 07:006 h
workWork in hand (working + blocked above 0)×1
workIdle, or paused×4
roomallowed, or unknown×1
roomallowed_warning×4
roomrejected, or overage in use×8

The band is the one at the time of the beat. next_by = at + interval.

Worked examples:

  • Tuesday 14:00, 3 sessions working, allowed: 30 min × 1 × 1 = 30 min.
  • Wednesday 10:00, idle, allowed: 30 min × 4 = 2 h.
  • Saturday 11:00, 2 working, allowed_warning: 2 h × 1 × 4 = 8 h.
  • Thursday 15:00, 1 working, rejected: 30 min × 8 = 4 h (and red).
  • Tuesday 03:00, idle, allowed: 6 h × 4 = 24 h, capped at 12 h.

3. The check: green, amber, red

From the last beat: due = at + interval (the check's own sum), and grace = a quarter of the interval, at least 15 minutes.

ColourWhen (the first that matches, from the top)
redThe file cannot be read or parsed, or v is unknown; or no file at all for a home marked required; or now is later than due + interval + grace (two beats missed); or state is stopped; or room is rejected or in overage while sessions are working (the account stops mid-work).
greyNo file yet for a home not yet marked required ("no heartbeat yet", during roll-out); or state is paused and the beat is not two beats late (paused on purpose). No alert.
amberNow is later than due + grace (one beat missed); or room is allowed_warning; or rejected or overage while idle; or room unknown; or state is blocked; or resets_at is earlier than at (stale); or at is more than 10 minutes in the future (a clock is wrong).
greenOtherwise.

Open asks for Dinis do not change the colour; the page shows the count and the link beside it.

4. Alerts

A red account is raised to Dinis. For now: at each wake I read every home's heartbeat and write one line per red account under "Dinis:" in my agent.json (account, why, since when), and remove it when the account is no longer red. Later: the same line goes as a needs-you item to his Chat space through a.drive's relay.

5. The code

  • heartbeat/heartbeat.mjs: the rule (interval), the check (judge), the alert lines (dinisLines) and the view (render, mount). One file, no dependencies, runs in a browser and in Node. The six homes are in HOMES; set a home's required to true once its maestro beats, so a missing file then turns red.
  • heartbeat/test.mjs: the worked examples above, every colour, British Summer Time, and the alert lines (node heartbeat/test.mjs).
  • heartbeat/run.mjs: my wake check; prints each home's colour and the "Dinis:" lines.

To put the view on /accounts/: copy heartbeat.mjs to site/accounts/heartbeat.mjs and add, where the table should go:

<div id="heartbeats">Reading the heartbeats...</div>
<script type="module">
import { mount } from './heartbeat.mjs';
mount(document.getElementById('heartbeats'));
</script>

It reads each home from raw.githubusercontent.com (cached there for up to 5 minutes, well inside the 30 minute minimum) and refreshes every 5 minutes. If the page ever gets a Content-Security-Policy, its connect-src must allow https://raw.githubusercontent.com.
